Morten Fredslund Løkke

Morten Fredslund Løkke is an and tech researcher based in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Work

mfloekke@live.dk

An ambient designer shapes atmosphere rather than objects. Like light and sound in a room, the work tints the physical and emotional climate of a space and lets the real world become its material. It holds many levels of attention without demanding any one: as ignorable as it is interesting, undemanding in form and open to paradox.
